## Title

Image Gallery Carousel with Lightbox

---

## Feature Overview

An Image Gallery section, placed in `headless-main`, shows authored images one at a time in a carousel. It has an optional `h2` heading, optional per-slide captions, previous/next controls, and slide indicators. Activating the visible slide opens it full-size in a modal lightbox. Visitors can step through the gallery there and close back to the carousel. In Sitecore editing mode the gallery becomes a static, fully expanded list so authors can reach every field. This note follows the approved specification (32 ACs) and does not change its scope.

---

## Design Intent

- **Experience objective:** a compact, calm way to browse a set of images inside a content page. Inspecting any image at full size takes one action, and the visitor never leaves the page.
- **Content first:** the image is the subject. Authored images are fitted within the frame, never cropped (letterboxed on a neutral surface), so authors' compositions are respected. Chrome (controls, indicators, counter) is kept small and neutral and sits outside the image so it never covers content.
- **Stability:** the gallery's footprint is constant while slides change: fixed frame ratio per breakpoint, and a caption area that reserves its height (AC7). The page never jumps.
- **Visual restraint:** neutral palette throughout. Primary colour is used only for the current-slide indicator and focus states. No shadows, gradients, or decorative transitions. A slide change is instant or a short fade, and becomes instant when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
- **Predictable interaction:** visual order matches focus order. Wrap-around behaves the same in the carousel and the lightbox, and a visible "x of y" position makes the wrap easy to follow.
- **Authoring parity:** the editing view uses the same spacing, typography, and frame language as the live view. It removes interactivity only, so authors still see a faithful sense of the content.

## Page / Feature Structure

The gallery is a single section in the `headless-main` placeholder. It follows the design system's Page → Section → Container → Content model and sits in the same centred content container as neighbouring authored sections. It is not full-bleed. Normal mode has these regions, top to bottom:

1. **Heading region:** optional `h2`, left-aligned to the container like other section headings.
2. **Carousel region:** a labelled region announced as a carousel. Its accessible name is the heading text when present, else "Image gallery". It contains:
   - **Slide frame:** the visible slide, a fixed-ratio frame acting as the lightbox trigger.
   - **Caption area:** the current slide's caption, below the frame.
   - **Control row:** previous control, slide indicators, next control, and a muted "x of y" counter.
   - **Status announcer:** a visually hidden polite live region for user-initiated slide changes.
3. **Lightbox overlay** (only while open): a full-viewport modal layered above the page, outside the section's visual flow.

Low-count variants: with one image, only the heading, frame, and caption render (no control row). With zero images in normal mode, nothing renders (AC16).

Editing mode replaces the carousel region with a **stacked list** of Gallery Image blocks. The always-editable heading stays above it. There is no control row, announcer, or lightbox (AC24–AC26).

## Section Breakdown

### Heading region

- **Purpose:** names the gallery as a section of the page and labels the carousel.
- **Content:** the authored Heading (Single-Line Text), rendered as `h2` (see Accessibility for heading-level reasoning).
- **Visual guidance:** design-system H2 scale (30–36px desktop, scaled down on mobile per the type stack). Standard grouping gap of 24 (`space-y-6`) to the carousel. Omitted entirely when empty in normal mode, with no reserved space.

### Slide frame

- **Purpose:** shows the current image. Opens the lightbox.
- **Content:** one image from the Sitecore Image field, `alt` from the image item.
- **Visual guidance:**
  - Fixed aspect ratio: 4:3 on mobile, 16:9 from tablet upward.
  - The image is fitted fully within the frame (contain). Unused space shows a subtle neutral surface tone from the neutral palette (no border, no shadow).
  - Corner treatment matches whatever the design system's existing image/card treatment is. No new radius is introduced.
- **Interaction:**
  - The whole frame is one focusable trigger with an accessible name such as "View image full size: {alt}", or "View image {x} of {y} full size" when alt is empty.
  - Hover gives a zoom-in cursor and a very slight tonal shift on the surface. Focus shows the standard design-system focus ring, drawn outside the frame edge so it is not hidden by the image.
  - Images for slides not shown are not loaded eagerly. Only the first slide's image is a priority load.

### Caption area

- **Purpose:** shows the current slide's caption.
- **Content:** the authored Caption (Single-Line Text). When the current slide has no caption, no caption element is rendered for it (AC5). The area still keeps its reserved height.
- **Visual guidance:**
  - Body or supporting text size (16px), muted supporting colour, left-aligned, at most 2 lines on narrow viewports with normal wrapping (no truncation).
  - 16 gap from the frame.
  - **The area reserves the height of the tallest authored caption in the gallery**, so moving between captioned, uncaptioned, and longer-captioned slides never changes the gallery height (AC7). If no slide has a caption, the area takes no space.

### Control row

- **Purpose:** move between slides and show the position.
- **Content (in visual and focus order):** Previous control, indicators (one per image), Next control. The muted "x of y" counter sits at the row's trailing end on tablet and desktop, and on its own line beneath the indicators on mobile.
- **Visual guidance:**
  - 16 gap below the caption area. The row is centred within the container.
  - **Previous/Next:** minimal icon buttons (simple chevron glyphs, no text) with neutral foreground and no filled background. Hover is a slight colour shift. Each has a minimum 44×44 target and an accessible name ("Previous slide" / "Next slide"). Neither is ever disabled, because the carousel wraps (AC10).
  - **Indicators:** small round dots inside at least 24×24 targets, spaced 8 apart. Inactive dots are neutral-outlined. The current dot is filled with the primary colour **and** visibly wider (pill shape), so the state is not conveyed by colour alone (AC11). Each is named "Go to slide n". The current one is exposed as current. When there are many images the row wraps onto additional centred lines rather than overflowing horizontally.
  - **Counter:** muted supporting text, "x of y".
- **Interaction:** Enter/Space activates controls. With focus anywhere inside the carousel region (frame or control row), Left/Right Arrow move to the previous/next slide with wrap (AC13). Focus stays on the element that had it, so pressing Next repeatedly keeps focus on Next. Changing slides through the controls updates the live announcer with "Slide x of y" (plus caption if present). Nothing is announced on initial load.

### Lightbox overlay

- **Purpose:** inspect one image at full size and step through the gallery.
- **Content:**
  - the image, requested at a size suited to full-viewport display rather than slide size
  - its caption, if authored
  - an "Image x of y" counter
  - a Close control
  - Previous/Next controls when there are two or more images
- **Visual guidance:**
  - A full-viewport dark neutral scrim at high opacity, the same in light and dark themes so photographs read consistently.
  - The image is centred and fitted (contain) within the viewport minus padding of at least 16 (mobile) or 32 (desktop), leaving room for the caption and controls. It is never upscaled beyond its intrinsic size.
  - Caption and counter sit directly below the image in light text on the scrim, centred and width-limited for readability. The counter uses muted light tone.
  - **Close** is a minimal icon button ("X" glyph) fixed at the top-right of the viewport.
  - **Previous/Next:** on tablet and desktop, minimal icon buttons vertically centred at the left and right viewport edges, outside the image bounds where space allows. On mobile they move to a bottom bar beneath the caption, so they don't cover the image and stay within thumb reach.
  - All lightbox controls use light foregrounds with at least 3:1 contrast against the scrim and a focus ring that is visible on dark (see Risks).
- **Interaction:**
  - Opens on activating the slide frame. Open and close are instant or a short fade, and instant under reduced motion.
  - On open, focus moves to Close. Tab and Shift+Tab cycle only within the lightbox. Left/Right Arrow step with wrap. Escape closes, and a click on the scrim closes. A click on the image, caption, counter, or controls does not close it (AC21).
  - Page scroll is locked while open.
  - On close, the carousel shows whichever image was last viewed in the lightbox, and focus returns to that slide's frame trigger (AC22).
  - Navigating inside the lightbox announces "Image x of y" through the lightbox's own polite status text.

### Editing-mode list

- **Purpose:** make every field reachable for inline editing (AC24–AC26).
- **Content:**
  - the heading field, always rendered, including when empty
  - one block per Gallery Image in authored order, each with its image field and its caption field (caption rendered as editable even when empty)
- **Visual guidance:**
  - Blocks stack vertically, 32 apart (`space-y-8`). Each block shows the image at full container width and in the same frame ratio and fit as the live carousel, with the caption 16 below it. Authors see how each image will sit in the live frame.
  - No controls, indicators, counter, or lightbox. Nothing is hidden.
- **Interaction:** none beyond the editor's own field editing. Image frames are not triggers.

## Component Recommendations

- **Section wrapper and content container:** reuse the existing section/container patterns used by other `headless-main` renderings so width, padding, and vertical rhythm match neighbouring sections. No gallery-specific container rules.
- **Typography block:** reuse the existing section-heading treatment for the `h2`, and the supporting-text style for captions and counters.
- **Field rendering:** use the Content SDK's field primitives for Text and Image, so the same output serves normal and editing modes and keeps inline editing. Image output should go through the Next.js-optimised image path, not a raw image element.
- **Interaction roles** (conceptual, for planning to map to components):
  - a static **gallery shell** (heading, mode switch, editing list) that has no client interactivity
  - a small **interactive carousel** that owns the current index, controls, indicators, keyboard handling, and announcer
  - a **lightbox dialog** that owns the open state, focus containment, scroll lock, and its own index, and shares the current index with the carousel on open and close

  This keeps the client surface limited to the interactive pieces (spec risk: client/server boundary).
- **Minimal icon button:** one shared visual pattern for Previous, Next, and Close in both surfaces (icon-only, accessible name, generous target, design-system focus ring), with a light-on-dark variant for the lightbox. Simple inline glyphs only. No icon library and no new npm dependencies (AC30).
- **Modal dialog pattern:** if the application already has a dialog primitive or the platform's native modal dialog behaviour is available, prefer it for dialog semantics and focus containment over a hand-rolled overlay. Either way it must meet AC18–AC23.
- **Promo Card Grid (T005) precedent:** follow its established approach to reading a Multilist of referenced data-source items (see Dependencies and Open Questions). Do not create a new data-access pattern.

## Responsive Behaviour

### Mobile

- Frame ratio is 4:3, at full container width with standard container padding.
- Heading uses the mobile step of the H2 scale. The caption area may wrap to 2 lines and reserves the tallest caption's height.
- Control row: Previous and Next flank the indicators. The counter sits centred on its own line beneath them. Indicators wrap onto additional centred lines when there are many images. Every target is at least 44×44 for previous/next and at least 24×24 for indicators.
- Lightbox: the image fills the available width with 16 padding. Close is at the top-right. Previous/Next sit in a bottom bar beneath the caption and counter.

### Tablet

- Frame ratio is 16:9, at full container width.
- The control row sits on one centred line with the counter at the trailing end. Spacing follows the same 16/24 rhythm.
- Lightbox: previous and next move to the vertically centred left and right edges. Padding is 32.

### Desktop

- Frame ratio is 16:9, bounded by the shared content container's maximum width. The gallery does not grow past the container on large screens.
- Same control row as tablet.
- Lightbox: the image is constrained by viewport height (minus caption and control space) as well as width, and never exceeds the image's intrinsic size.

### Cross-breakpoint rules

- No horizontal overflow at any width, in the indicator row or anywhere else.
- Gallery height is constant within a breakpoint while slides change. It changes only when the breakpoint (and therefore the ratio) changes.
- Visual order equals focus order at every breakpoint.
- Hierarchy is preserved: heading → image → caption → controls.

## Accessibility Considerations

- **Heading level (carry-forward 1):** `h2` is the correct level for this component as a section within a page whose `h1` is owned by the page or Hero Banner. The gallery never renders an `h1` and has no subheadings, so it cannot skip levels internally. **I could not verify this against existing components**, because source files and prior task artifacts are outside this stage's input contract. The result is recorded as unverified, and planning must check it (Open Questions 1). If no existing component or page shell provides an `h1`, that is a page-level gap and not a reason to change the gallery's level. Per the spec, any conflict is raised, not solved by adding a field.
- **Carousel semantics:** a labelled region announced as a carousel, named by the heading or "Image gallery". Hidden slides are removed from the accessibility tree and tab order (AC14). The position is exposed as "Slide x of y". Only user-initiated changes are announced, politely. There is no autoplay.
- **Lightbox semantics:**
  - Modal dialog named by caption, else heading, else "Image gallery".
  - Focus moves to Close on open, is contained while open, and returns to the originating (or last-viewed) slide trigger on close.
  - Background content is inert and the page scroll is locked.
- **Keyboard:** Tab order follows the visual order (frame trigger, previous, indicators, next). Enter/Space activate all controls. Arrow keys work within the carousel and the lightbox. Escape closes the lightbox. No control becomes disabled, so focus is never lost to a disabled element.
- **Focus visibility:** use the design-system focus style on every interactive element. Inside the lightbox, the focus indicator must stay clearly visible against the dark scrim.
- **Contrast and non-colour cues:**
  - Control glyphs and indicator outlines meet 3:1 non-text contrast against their background (page surface, or scrim in the lightbox).
  - Caption text meets 4.5:1. Muted text must still pass, including light-on-scrim in the lightbox.
  - The current indicator is distinguished by shape as well as colour.
- **Targets:** at least 44×44 for previous, next, and close. At least 24×24 for indicators, with 8 spacing.
- **Text alternatives:** `alt` comes from the image item. The trigger's accessible name adds the "view full size" purpose. Captions are visible text and are not duplicated into alt.
- **Motion:** slide and lightbox transitions are minimal and become instant under the reduced-motion preference.
- **Hard-coded labels:** "Previous slide", "Next slide", "Go to slide n", "Slide x of y", "Image x of y", "View image full size", "Close", and "Image gallery" are English-only until B012. Keep them grouped and consistent so they are easy to swap for dictionary values later.

## Risks

- **Undefined patterns in the design system:** there is no carousel, modal or lightbox, icon-button, scrim, or aspect-ratio guidance. This note derives them from existing principles (restraint, neutrals, minimal buttons, focus states). They need confirming (Open Questions 3).
- **Focus ring on dark scrim:** the standard focus style may lack contrast on the lightbox scrim. A light-on-dark variant of the same focus treatment may be needed.
- **Caption height reservation:** reserving the tallest caption's height means one unusually long caption adds whitespace under every slide. This is acceptable because the field is Single-Line Text, but authors should be steered to short captions in the CMS guide.
- **Many images:** indicators wrap to multiple lines on mobile when a gallery is large (roughly 12 or more images), which adds visual weight. There is no authored limit in scope.
- **Letterboxing:** fit-within (contain) avoids cropping, but strongly portrait images in a 16:9 frame will appear small with wide side bars. This is the deliberate trade-off for stability and content fidelity (no crop and no focal-point control in scope).
- **Editing-mode data shape:** if referenced Gallery Image fields come through without editing metadata, inline editing of images and captions (AC24) would fail even though the live view works.
- **Unverified carry-forwards:** heading ownership and Multilist resolution are resolved by design rationale only. Planning must confirm both before Build.
