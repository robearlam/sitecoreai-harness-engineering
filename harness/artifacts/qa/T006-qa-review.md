## Design Alignment Review

**Matches well**

- Structure follows the design: section and container match Promo Card Grid. Then the `h2`, the frame, the caption area, the control row (previous, indicators, next, counter) and a hidden polite announcer.
- The client boundary has three roles: a server shell with the editing list, a client carousel, and a client lightbox in a portal.
- Ratios are 4:3, then 16:9 from `md`, contain fit on `neutral-100`, and the caption height is reserved. The counter wraps on mobile.
- The pill-shaped current indicator has a 24px target. The 44×44 icon buttons share one component with a dark variant.
- The lightbox has a dark scrim, Close top-right, a white focus outline, caption and counter below the image, and no upscaling. There are no transitions, so reduced motion is met.
- **Rework alignment:** the design says "focus stays on the element that had it". The rework keeps that for the controls. For the trigger, focus moves to the new slide's trigger, because the old one is hidden. This is the closest faithful behaviour and it is what round 1 recommended.

**Partially aligned / deviations (non-blocking)**

- Primary accent never renders (issue 3).
- Lightbox prev/next sit in a bottom row at every breakpoint, where the design puts them at the left and right edges on tablet and desktop. The frame has no hover tonal shift (issue 10).
- The background is not inert (issue 11).
- The design system has no carousel, modal, icon-button, scrim or aspect-ratio patterns (design note Open Question 3). That is not a defect of this task.
- A page without a Hero Banner has no `h1`. That is a page-composition gap recorded by the plan, not a gallery defect.

## Issues Identified

No blocking issues. The round-1 blocking issue (arrow-key focus loss, AC13) is fixed in code and has a discriminating regression test. Its real-browser confirmation is listed above.

1. **Live region not refreshed after the lightbox changes the index** (`galleryCarousel.tsx:66-70`, correctness-pass #4). Confirmed. `closeLightbox` sets the index without touching `announcement`. Sequence: press Next (announces "Slide 2 of 3. Caption 2"), open the lightbox, step to 3, close (carousel on 3, live text unchanged), press Previous. `goTo(1)` writes the identical string, React makes no DOM change, and the polite region stays silent. Visually the counter updates. This is not from the rework; `closeLightbox` is unchanged since 2735474.  
   Severity: Medium  
   Blocks approval: No. It needs a specific multi-step sequence that returns to exactly the last-announced slide, after a lightbox navigation. Every other user-initiated change is announced. Fix: clear `announcement` when the lightbox opens or closes.

2. **`npm run lint` does not lint any component file** (`package.json` `"lint": "eslint ./src/**/*.tsx ./src/**/*.ts"`). Under `/bin/sh` the unquoted `**` matches only one directory level.  
   Severity: Medium  
   Blocks approval: No. The defect is pre-existing and not caused by this task, and the command does not fail. Build's direct ESLint run is the substitute evidence.

3. **`primary` colour utilities are dead** (`galleryCarousel.tsx:104,149,155`, `galleryIconButton.tsx:27`). No `primary` colour is defined in the Tailwind v4 setup.  
   Severity: Medium  
   Blocks approval: No. The current indicator is still distinguished by shape, and focus outlines fall back to `currentColor`. A project-level primary token is a design-system decision.

4. **Duplicate heading id with two galleries on a page** (`ImageGallery.tsx:7`, correctness-pass #1). Confirmed, unchanged since round 1.  
   Severity: Medium  
   Blocks approval: No. The specified single-gallery case is correct. Fix: derive the id from the rendering uid or data source id.

5. **Items with an empty or unpublished Image become blank slides** (`ImageGallery.tsx:15`, correctness-pass #2). Confirmed, unchanged.  
   Severity: Medium  
   Blocks approval: No. It needs an author error or unpublished media, outside normal use. Fix: filter on `fields.Image?.value?.src` in normal mode only.

6. **Lightbox image relies on field dimensions** (`galleryLightbox.tsx:121-125`, correctness-pass #3). Confirmed, unchanged. With no `fill` and no width or height, `next/image` throws when the field has no dimensions.  
   Severity: Medium  
   Blocks approval: No. Media Library raster images, the in-scope source, carry dimensions. Dimensionless sources are outside normal use.

7. **Arrow handlers ignore modifier keys** (`galleryCarousel.tsx:55-63`, `galleryLightbox.tsx:54-59`, correctness-pass #5). Confirmed in part. Alt/Ctrl/Meta+Arrow are `preventDefault`ed and change the slide, which can swallow browser Back (Alt+Left) while focus is in the carousel, or anywhere while the lightbox is open. The screen-reader part is mostly overstated: NVDA and JAWS browse-mode and VoiceOver VO-key commands are intercepted by the screen reader before the page sees them. That still needs the manual screen-reader pass.  
   Severity: Low  
   Blocks approval: No. AC13 and AC20 behaviour is correct. It only affects modifier shortcuts pressed while focus is in the widget or the modal is open. Fix: return early when `altKey || ctrlKey || metaKey`.

8. **Redundant announcement on arrow navigation from the trigger** (`galleryCarousel.tsx:57`, correctness-pass #6). Confirmed as a consequence of the rework. Focus moving to the new trigger announces its name, and the polite region then announces "Slide x of y[. caption]". The texts differ, so this is verbose rather than an identical double read.  
   Severity: Low  
   Blocks approval: No. Before the rework the trigger path broke after one press, so nothing that worked has regressed. AC12 and AC13 are both met. Tuning needs the screen-reader pass (for example, skip the live update when focus moved).

9. **Backdrop-close triggers on non-backdrop gestures** (`galleryLightbox.tsx:88-94,141`). Clicking the gap between the lightbox nav buttons, or a drag-select ending on the backdrop, closes it.  
   Severity: Low  
   Blocks approval: No. These are edge gestures. The image, caption and controls behave as AC21 requires.

10. **Lightbox control placement, frame hover and arbitrary value differ from the design.**  
    Severity: Low  
    Blocks approval: No. Small design deviations that break no AC.

11. **Background not made inert while the lightbox is open** (`galleryLightbox.tsx`).  
    Severity: Low  
    Blocks approval: No. AC19 focus containment is met. Virtual-cursor behaviour needs the manual pass.

12. **Captions are raw strings in normal mode** (`galleryCarousel.tsx:128`, `galleryLightbox.tsx:128`).  
    Severity: Low  
    Blocks approval: No. Normal mode has no editing chrome, and the editing path uses `Text`.

13. **Index not clamped when `items` shrinks** (`galleryCarousel.tsx:23`).  
    Severity: Low

14. **Deprecated `priority` and redundant `loading="lazy"`** (`galleryCarousel.tsx:110-111`, correctness-pass #7, in part). Confirmed: Next 16.2.3 marks `priority` `@deprecated` in favour of `preload` (types only, no runtime warning). `loading="lazy"` repeats next/image's default.
